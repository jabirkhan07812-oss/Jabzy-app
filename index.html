JABZY Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JABZY collects, uses, and shares information when you use the app.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use JABZY, information may include your account details and authentication information; username, age, bio, and profile photo; messages and photos you send; stories, captions, comments, and story interactions; likes, follows, matches, and other app activity; and reports, feedback, and help requests you submit.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, show profiles and app features, deliver messages and stories, operate social interactions, respond to support requests, investigate reports, enforce our rules, and maintain the safety and operation of JABZY.

3. What other users can see

Your username, profile details, profile photo, and content you choose to post may be visible to other users according to the app's features and settings. Stories are currently stored using publicly accessible media URLs; anyone who obtains a story media URL may be able to view that media. Do not post anything you want to keep private. Messages are intended for the people in the conversation, but do not treat online communications as a guarantee of absolute confidentiality.

4. Service providers

JABZY uses Supabase for services such as authentication, database, and file storage. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices. If you choose a third-party sign-in option, that provider may also process information under its own policy.

5. Safety, reports, and administrators

Authorised JABZY administrators may access information needed to review user reports, feedback, help requests, and moderation or safety concerns. Please do not include passwords or highly sensitive personal information in messages or support requests.

6. Storage and security

We use the app's service providers and available access controls to store and protect information. No online service can guarantee perfect security. Keep your password private and tell us if you believe your account has been compromised.

7. Retention and deletion requests

Information is kept while needed to operate the app, meet safety and legal needs, and resolve disputes. Some content, backups, or records may remain for a period after a deletion request where technically or legally necessary. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact us at jabzyofficial@gmail.com.

8. Age restriction

JABZY is intended for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly intend to provide the service to anyone under 18. If you believe an underage person has created an account, contact us.

9. Your choices

You can choose what information and content you post. You may contact us to ask questions or make a privacy-related request. App features and settings may vary as JABZY develops.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version and its update date will be shown here.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email jabzyofficial@gmail.com.

This is a draft policy based on the app's current features. Review it for legal compliance and update it whenever data practices or providers change.
